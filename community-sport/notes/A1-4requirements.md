# Instructions

The research report allows you to explore cutting-edge intersections of AI and web development while demonstrating your ability to critically analyse and reflect on emerging technologies in the field.

## Choose a Topic

For this component of the assignment, you are required to explore and write a **research report with five points** on **one of** the following topics:

**Topic A: Opportunities and Challenges of Using GenAI for Designing Data Models and Database Structures**: 

- Reflect on **five** key aspects of using Generative AI tools in the context of database design and data modelling for software applications.

**OR**

**Topic B: Strategies for Using GenAI in JavaScript Debugging**: 

- Explore and analyse **five** strategies for leveraging Generative AI to enhance the debugging process in JavaScript development.

**OR**

**Topic C: Opportunities and Challenges of Using GenAI for UX Innovation**: 

- Reflect on **five** key aspects of using Generative AI tools in the context of UX Innovation.

**OR**

**Topic D: Strategies for Using GenAI in Progressive Web Applications optimisation**: 

- Explore and analyse **five** strategies for leveraging Generative AI to enhance security in progressive web applications optimisation.

## Word count: Approximately 600 words

- There is an allocated word count of 600 words. (excluding references)
- Please note that there is a 10% grace limit, allowing for submissions of up to 700 words without incurring any penalties. 
- However, be aware that exceeding this grace limit (700 words) will result in a 10% penalty for every additional 100 words submitted beyond the limit. i.e. a 700 word essay will be capped at 90%.

## Report Structure

*Suggested word count range per section provided in parenthesis.*

1. Introduction (50-100 words): 
2. - Briefly introduce your chosen topic
   - Explain why this topic is relevant to modern web development
3. Main Body (400-500 words)
4. - Discuss five distinct points related to your chosen topic
   - For each point:
   - - Provide a clear explanation
     - Include relevant examples or use cases
     - **Critically analyze** potential benefits and drawbacks
5. Reflection (100-150 words)
6. - Synthesize your findings
   - Discuss the overall impact of GenAI on the chosen area of web development
   - Consider ethical implications or future trends
7. Conclusion (50-100 words)
8. - Summarize key takeaways
   - Suggest areas for further research or development
9. Acknowledgement of AI use
10. - Include clear documentation of how you used AI (see more instructions below)
11. References (IEEE Style)
